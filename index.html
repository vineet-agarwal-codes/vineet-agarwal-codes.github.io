mise

Shape your spaces.

Mise gives each place in your home a job. Stick an NFC tag on your desk, by your bed or at the front door, and tap your phone on it: social apps go quiet for deep work, sleep mode starts at night, and your morning routine appears when you wake.

How it works

Pick a recipe (Deep work for your desk, Sleep for your bed), adjust it if you like, and write it to any NFC sticker. Each tap switches the space on or off. No tag yet? Start any space from the app.

Mise is free. Android first; iPhone is coming.
