Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. Applies to the Mise app for Android (package app.mise).

Who we are

Mise is published on Google Play by its developer as listed on the Mise store page. For any privacy question or request, contact us at vineet140502@gmail.com or +91 7017223071.

What Mise collects

Mise works without an account. To rank recipes by how many people use them, the app sends a small amount of anonymous usage data, at most every six hours:

  • A random device ID created by the app on first launch. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Google account or advertising ID.
  • The IDs of the recipes you have added (for example desk-deep-work).
  • For each day, how many times each recipe was used (tag taps and starts from the app), with your time zone so days are counted correctly.
  • For each day, how many times a running mode stopped a blocked app from opening. Only the count is sent, never which app.

Mise does not collect your name, contacts, location, messages, photos, the list of apps on your phone, which apps you block, or your browsing.

What stays on your phone

Your spaces, their settings, your tags, the apps you choose to block and your detailed tap history stay on your phone. Usage access (to see which app is open) and the screen overlay are used only on the phone to cover a blocked app while a mode you started is on; nothing about those apps is sent anywhere.

How it's used and shared

The anonymous counts are combined across all users to show figures like "Used by 320 people this week" and to order recipes in Discover. We don't sell data, show ads, or share data with anyone else. The data is stored by our database provider, Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India), which processes it only on our behalf. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long it's kept

Daily usage counts are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. The list of recipes added on a device is replaced with each report and kept until you ask us to delete it. Aggregated totals (such as weekly user counts per recipe) contain no device IDs.

Your choices

To have the data for your device deleted, email us your device ID, shown in the app under any space → About. Uninstalling the app stops all collection; a reinstall creates a new, unrelated device ID.

Children

Mise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
